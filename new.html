🌾 The Problem

Betel leaf yield can be affected by environmental conditions and soil nutrients. Farmers may find it difficult to combine these factors into a consistent, data-informed estimate of expected production.

🤖 Our Proposed Solution

The research investigates an AI-based yield prediction approach using farm and climate variables. The intended system can present an estimate and useful context through a simple farmer-facing interface.

📱 Farmer-Centred Data Collection

The proposed mobile workflow uses manual entry of readings collected with suitable instruments, rather than requiring permanently installed IoT sensors in every field.

How the Proposed System Works

A simple workflow from farm readings to an informed decision.

  1. Collect readings: record soil moisture, temperature, humidity, NPK, pH and rainfall.
  2. Validate the data: check values, units and collection time before storing them.
  3. Prepare model inputs: apply the same feature names, units and preprocessing used during model training.
  4. Predict yield: send the prepared inputs to the trained XGBoost model.
  5. Present results: show the estimated leaf count per bed and explain the result clearly.
  6. Support decisions: use the prediction and verified agricultural guidance to help assess crop conditions.
